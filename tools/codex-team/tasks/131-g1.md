# Agent G1: canonical vessel geometry and the shared volume model (all experiments, validated on experiment 2 first)

Read `docs/AGCL_INTERFACES.md` (WAVE 14 OVERRIDE, ownership and the interface contract), `lib/vesselGeometry.ts` (the interface stub the coordinator created: you implement it; its signatures are the contract with Agent G2 and must not change without a note in your report), `lib/tubeLiquidLevel.ts`, `lib/tubeSilverGeometry.ts`, `docs/night/wave13c7/REPORT.md` (the discrepancy was discovered there), `tests/measure-model.mjs` (how the authored GLB is measured) and the experiment code that uses the geometry (`lib/tubeReactionVisuals.ts`, `lib/tubeAmounts.ts`, `lib/tubeVolumeField.ts` `LiquidMask`, the copper, barium and permanganate fields, `ui/PourStream.tsx`).

## The problem (customer: highest priority)
The game computes the visible liquid height with `tubeLiquidHeight(volumeMl, tubeEffectiveRadius(authoredHeight))`, where `tubeEffectiveRadius` solves for the radius at which the 2.5 mL default fills the authored 55 mm cavity (comment: "Game millilitres are calibrated to the authored 2.5 ml meniscus"), about 3.85 mm: 2 mL then stands about 43 mm high. The reaction and flow grids (`LiquidMask`, `TubeSilverField`, the other experiments' fields) use the real cavity radius read from the authored mesh bounding box (`radius = min(size.x,size.z)/2`, 7 mm for the silver tube: `SILVER_TUBE_RADIUS = 0.007`, `SILVER_TUBE_HEIGHT = 0.055`). With radius 7 mm, 2 mL would stand about 13 mm high. Volume, visible level, the cells the chemistry occupies and the flow geometry therefore disagree: the grid represents a liquid roughly 3.4 times larger than the stored volume, per-cell amounts are normalised fractions and do not know it, jet and entrainment calibrations were done in a 20 to 38 mm deep layer instead of 7 to 14 mm, and the reference video's level looks "several times lower" than the game's. Do not assume either radius is correct, and do not choose numbers to imitate the reference video.
