## What is asked
The incoming NaCl stream (about 1 mL in 1.2 to 1.7 s, jet radius about 1.15 mm, speed on the order of 0.25 to 1.5 m/s at the surface, entering at the real impact point) must stir the receiving 1 to 2 mL of AgNO3 solution through entrainment of surrounding liquid, deformation of concentration interfaces, strain and shear, and recirculation. In the current game the tracer stays a single smooth lobe near the wall and 61 percent of the fine mass is more than 3 mm from the impact at 9 s only because of slow drift. Establish whether the solver represents the jet correctly, and if not, add a limited, physically coupled local jet model. No fake pouring animation, no new mass.

1. **Diagnostics of the existing flow** (replay of the real stepped delivery events exactly as `tests/night/wave11c3/mixing.test.ts` does, aggregation off): the actual impact position, incoming momentum flux (rho Q v) versus what the solver injects (`jetStrength`, the `SurfaceSpread` closure `u_r = alpha Q/(2 pi r delta)[1-exp(-(r/r0)^2)] exp(-z/delta)`, the contact-patch injection), downward penetration depth over time, lateral entrainment (volume flux crossing a control surface around the jet, compared with the textbook plunging-jet entrainment estimate), velocity magnitude fields and the strain-rate and vorticity fields, recirculation (is there a closed circulation? where?), the flow near the wall (the jet sits 0.5 mm from it: wall-attached jet or Coanda-like behaviour, boundary conditions: no-slip or free-slip), the resolution (cell 0.9 mm horizontally and 4.6 mm vertically) and the numerical smoothing of the velocity (projection, viscous closure, `eddyScale`). Show with numbers where the jet momentum disappears.
2. **Passive tracer only** (`globalThis.__silverDiag = "tracer"`: precipitation off, only the incoming NaCl is traced, enters at the real impact point and is advected by the same velocity field the chemistry uses). Measure the tracer deformation: connected components, surface-to-volume ratio of the 10 percent and 50 percent iso-regions, interface area growth over time, the fraction mixed, vertical and horizontal second moments, penetration depth and spread rates. State whether it is one smooth blob, and why.
3. **Improve only what the evidence shows is wrong.** First candidates in order: a local jet model (momentum source distribution along the jet axis below the surface instead of only at the contact patch, with the jet penetrating to a depth set by momentum balance and decaying by entrainment: the classical plunging-jet or turbulent round-jet entrainment law gives a volume flux that grows with depth), conservative subgrid entrainment coupled to the resolved flow (a turbulent eddy viscosity from strain, Smagorinsky style, with a jet turbulence source at the entry point, acting in the momentum equation, bounded and positive), an improved velocity reconstruction at the faces (so strain at the unresolved scale is not lost to the projection), then boundary-condition changes near the wall. Do not replace the whole solver with a high-resolution one. Any new term must be coupled to the resolved flow, create no mass (divergence stays within the existing tolerance), be stable with the fixed 50 ms step, and be bounded. Report CPU cost per step (p50 and p95, HIGH 16x32x16 and LOW 12x24x12, 200 steps after 20 warm-up) with the flag off, and on.
