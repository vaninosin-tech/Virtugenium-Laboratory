## The physics to respect (customer correction)
A low simultaneous Ag+ and Cl- overlap does not by itself prove insufficient mixing: the reaction is fast, so the two dissolved reactants are consumed as soon as they meet. Look at the transport fluxes that bring them into contact, and separate physical mixing limits from immediate chemical consumption. Do not use the visual reference to impose a reaction extent at a given time (the reference video has changes of playback speed and is not a calibrated reaction clock).
