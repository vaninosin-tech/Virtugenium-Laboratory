## Situation (read carefully)
1. In the real game today NOTHING white is drawn for the fine suspension: `tubeReactionVisuals.ts` never sets `uCloudOn`, and `uSmokeOn` is 0 (the customer switched the smoke layer off), so with large-floc aggregation disabled (`globalThis.__silverAgg = 0`, Diagnostic C) the tube looks clear while the dumps show `suspensionMass` up to 0.23. The cloud branch exists only in `tubeSilverVolumeMaterial.ts` near `uCloudOn` (extinction = `uCloudK * c`, with c = smokeValue * pow(fbm noise * 1.9, 2.2)). That branch modulates the density with a procedural fbm, which is the main source of its "structure". The customer explicitly forbids procedural noise as the source of large lobes, branches or hanging formations.
2. Physics of the look: AgCl suspension is white because small particles SCATTER light. Extinction alone gives a grey cloud. Use `T(s) = exp(-integral sigma_t ds)` and `L_out = T_total * L_background + L_scattered`, with sigma_t proportional to the sampled fine concentration and the scattered term computed from the field itself (single scattering plus a cheap multiple-scattering approximation is enough, for example: in-scattered light from a light direction using 2 or 3 optical-depth taps toward the light, an albedo near 1 for AgCl, a forward-leaning phase function with an isotropic floor, plus an ambient term that rises with local optical thickness so thick regions saturate to white while thin regions stay translucent). Do not add AgCl mass. Do not use extra simulated mass or global brightening.
3. Procedural noise is allowed only as SMALL-scale optical texture (sub-cell grain modulating sigma_t by a bounded amount, spatial frequency above the field resolution) so the cloud does not look like interpolated grid blobs. It must not create lobes or branches larger than a grid cell. Large structure must come from the field (Agent C1 improves it).
4. Preserve glass and liquid depth relationships (draw order, `wet()` clipping to the liquid, discard outside), the Diagnostic A tracer and the occupied-cell mode (`uDiag`), and the sediment and floc paths. Experiments 1, 3, 4 must render pixel-identically (this material is used only by experiment 2; verify with a test that the other experiments do not reference it).
