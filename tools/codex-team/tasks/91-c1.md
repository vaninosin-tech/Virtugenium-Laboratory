## Situation
Diagnostic C is stage 3 with large-floc aggregation disabled (`globalThis.__silverAgg = 0`, see `advanceFlocSystem` in `lib/tubeSilverField.ts`): the only solid is the fine suspension `suspension[0]` (Ag+ and Cl- precipitate in `precipitatePair`, `productSource`, and `formed` is added to `suspension[0]` at the `flocMode` line `this.suspension[0][i] += formed`). It is transported by `this.solver.transport(this.suspension[k], step, diffusivity, eddyScale)` (`lib/tubeFlowSolver.ts:183`: staggered flow, advection plus diffusion plus the plunging-jet closure). The renderer samples `pixels = suspension[0] * activeCount` (3D texture `texture`, uniform `uHaze`). Customer verdict: the rendered cloud is too smooth, no dense cores, no branching, no transparent gaps, too uniform grey. The chemistry source, the Ag+/Cl- transport and the delivery architecture are validated and must not change in meaning.

## Your job: find out where structure is lost and fix transport, never the image
1. **Stage-by-stage comparison (required evidence).** For a deterministic replay of the real pour (drive `TubeSilverRuntime`/`TubeSilverField` exactly as `tests/night/wave10diag` and `tests/night/wave9runtime` do, or replay the dumps), record for every 50 ms step: (A) raw `formed` source per cell, (B) `suspension[0]` before and after `solver.transport`, (C) `pixels` as given to the renderer. Quantify structure with scale-free measures: total variation, coefficient of variation among liquid cells that hold material, the number of connected components above thresholds (26-connectivity), the ratio of the 95th to the 50th percentile of nonzero cells, the fraction of variance above 2 cells wavelength. Show them for each stage and say where the numbers collapse.
2. **Numerical diffusion audit.** Identify the advection scheme in `TubeFlowSolver.transport` (first-order upwind? semi-Lagrangian? interpolation order?). Measure its numerical diffusion with a passive tracer advected by a known flow (rigid translation and a sharp step or slotted cylinder across a few cells) and with the actual flow from the dumps; report effective numerical diffusivity against the explicit `diffusivity` (0.000012 m2/s for the fine class, plus the `eddyScale` eddy diffusion). Say which of explicit diffusion, eddy closure, scheme diffusion, or grid resolution (16 x 32 x 16 for HIGH, 12 x 24 x 12 for LOW) dominates the loss of structure.
3. **Resolution.** A grid cell is about 0.9 mm and the liquid is only a few cells deep. Test what the structure measures do at 2x resolution of the SAME scalar only (the flow stays on the coarse grid and is interpolated): a limited finer representation of the fine-suspension concentration and its source, advected with the interpolated coarse velocity, conservative (flux form), without multiplying the cost of the Ag+/Cl- fields. If it clearly helps, implement it as an optional additional array and a NEW optional 3D texture on the field: `fineTexture: THREE.Data3DTexture` (RedFormat, FloatType, same value convention as `texture`: concentration times the coarse `activeCount`, so the domain mean is the same) with `fineTextureSize: {nx,ny,nz}`. The renderer reads `field.fineTexture ?? field.texture` with the same normalised domain mapping, so existing consumers keep working. Profile CPU ms per step (HIGH and LOW) before and after; do not ship a change that raises the mean step cost by more than 40 percent unless the structure gain is large and measured.
4. **Fix the actual cause.** Candidate causes to test one at a time, with evidence: excessive eddy or explicit diffusion, first-order upwind, the contact-patch injection weights, coarse injection of `formed` into the containing cell, nearest-cell redistribution on mask changes, the assembly of `pixels` (`opticalMix`, `suspensionOpticalScale` and the lines that blend toward the spatial mean; check whether the stage 3 branch is affected). Keep what is physical: a documented, bounded explicit diffusivity is fine; a less diffusive conservative scheme (second-order limited, or monotone semi-Lagrangian with flux correction) is fine; sub-cell tracers are fine if conservative. Not allowed: noise injected into the source, drawing branches, normalising or globally spreading the field to make it whiter, changing the Ag+/Cl- transport or source, changing the delivery.
5. **Momentum check.** Verify with numbers that the incoming stream momentum and the buoyancy actually deform the suspension (centroid and second moments of the suspension versus a run with the jet closure disabled and a run with no buoyancy), that the cloud follows the liquid velocity (correlation of suspension displacement with the velocity field) and that nothing moves without a velocity.
6. **Conservation.** Total formed AgCl (`extent`) equals fine suspension plus explicitly accounted transfers (aggregation off: zero; sediment off for the diagnostic, or accounted) to 1e-12 at every step, also across mask changes and for the new fine representation. Tests in `tests/night/wave10c1/`, deterministic, exact numbers. Do not edit existing expected values; if old tests break, report whether it is a confirmed regression or pre-existing (known pre-existing failures: `tests/night/wave4a/field.test.ts`, `tests/night/wave4b/PourStream.test.ts`, `tests/night/wave6perf/flocPerf.test.ts` golden totals near line 76; say whether your change alters them).
7. **Evidence files (you have no browser).** Write node scripts that render the numeric states to image frames without WebGL (PPM or raw RGB files; the coordinator converts with ffmpeg): (a) concentration slice sequences (central vertical plane and a horizontal plane) of `suspension[0]` as grayscale, (b) local source `formed` slices, (c) velocity magnitude and vectors slice, (d) side-by-side before and after of the same replay. Save under `docs/night/wave10c1/` with the numbers in `REPORT.md`: the main cause of the smoothing, structure measures per stage before and after, CPU ms per step before and after (HIGH and LOW, p50 and p95 over at least 200 steps), the exact public API additions.
