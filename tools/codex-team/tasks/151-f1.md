## What to build
Three conserved states of the same AgCl: `M_total = M_fine + M_aggregated + M_sediment`, with `M_total` equal to the formed AgCl (`extent`) at every step to 1e-12 (including during the pour, across mask changes and after the level rises), and transfers that never duplicate or lose material. Do not render or store the same mass twice: while mass is in an aggregate it is not in the fine field and the reverse.
1. **Aggregate formation** from the existing suspended material, never prefabricated: modeled aggregation (Smoluchowski-style, rate rising with local fine-particle concentration squared, with the shear and sticking efficiency you can justify; the existing floc code uses `FLOC_AGGREGATION` and `FLOC_C0`: recalibrate for the corrected geometry where the layer is 7 to 14 mm deep and concentrations are higher; document every constant) that moves mass from `suspension[0]` into the aggregate representation at the local cells where concentration and collision conditions are met. The aggregate representation is the existing cohesive `FlocSystem` (nodes with plastic links, fractal dimension about 1.9, bounded node budget with merging) or an improved version of it: variable size; irregular connected structure; uneven density; loose internal arrangement; partial merging and separation (breakage by shear is allowed by `__silverBreak`: decide whether it should now be on by default in stage 3 and justify it with replays); non-identical settling (slip speed from drag and buoyancy balance with solid fraction `size^(Df-3)`, so large loose aggregates settle at their own speed and small ones stay suspended); some remain suspended while others settle. Forbidden: rigid rocks, spheres, snowflakes, sand grains, predefined fall animations, teleporting, identical settling speeds, pinning aggregates to the surface, a fixed timer deciding anything, stirring the whole volume artificially.
2. **Aggregate dynamics** coupled to the liquid: aggregates are advected by the existing resolved velocity field (`TubeFlowSolver`; the incoming liquid must be able to deform and carry them: measure that a flow-less run keeps them nearly in place while the real-pour run displaces them), with feedback of their weight onto the liquid as the existing code does (`loadWeight`), erosion of loose pieces back into the fine suspension (conserved), compaction by load (never by age), and the node budget respected (no per-frame allocations that grow; report the maximum node count and memory).
3. **Sedimentation**: aggregates reaching the actual vessel bottom (`tubeVessel` bowl and the field mask's bottom, not a 55 mm assumption) transfer their mass into `SilverSedimentField`: grows from arriving mass only, irregular and soft, curdy not sandy (columns with angle of repose, local slumping and compaction as the existing class does, check it at the 7 mm radius scale and the real number of columns), progressive accumulation, no fixed-timer sediment. The game's chemistry units are normalized (1.0 means the AgCl formed from the 1 mL NaCl portion), so the conversion from conserved mass to deposit height needs ONE explicit documented constant (the bulk volume of loose settled AgCl per unit mass, with your derivation from the density of AgCl 5.56 g/cm3, an assumed loose-packing porosity and the molarity implied by the game, and a statement of the uncertainty); never scale the mass itself. Report the resulting deposit height in millimetres in the 7 mm tube at the end of the experiment and compare with the reference qualitatively (the reference vessel is wider: compare the volume fraction, not pixels).
4. **Regression discipline**: with aggregation off (`__silverAgg = 0`) the field must remain bit-identical to the accepted baseline (the existing replay tests `tests/night/wave11c3`, `wave12c6`, `wave13c7`, `wave10diag` and the field tests prove it; do not re-pin their numbers). Decide with evidence and write down how the default is selected (aggregation on by default in stage 3 from now on? The coordinator will confirm: provide a switch `window.__silverAgg` (0 off, anything else on) and report both modes). Experiments 1, 3, 4 untouched (the solver and `tubeSilverField.ts` are silver-only; keep `tubePrecipitationPhysics.ts` additions additive).
