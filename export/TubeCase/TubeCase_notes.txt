TubeCase.glb — sealed storage case for 32 test tubes (source: blender-source/TubeCase_Sealed_V2.blend)
Footprint 300 x 220 mm, 230 mm tall closed. Origin: centre of the base. Unit: metres, Y up in the glb.

THREE MECHANISMS (all driven by the model's own animation clips, one shared 5 s timeline, 120 frames @ 24 fps)

1) FLAT FOLDING HANDLE  (Lid_Handle + Lid_Handle_B; Handle_Pivot / Handle_Pivot_B; frames 1..55)
   Two flat halves (together the shape of the whole lid recess, 130 x 80 mm) on ONE steel axis (Handle_Pin) that runs along
   the centre line of the lid, so the raised handle stands over the middle of the case (balanced carry). Folded, each half
   lies flush in its half of the recess; raised, they stand side by side as a double flat grip with aligned finger windows.
   The halves hang below the axis and fold away from each other, so they never pass through one another.
   Hardware: two brackets standing on the recess floor (Handle_Brackets, bored for the axis), a torsion spring on each
   side of the axis (Handle_Springs: it is what lifts the halves a little when released and holds them against the stop —
   modelled as geometry only, the game plays the motion), hinge knuckles interleaved (A: centre, B: ends).
   Handle_Pivot rotates about its local X:   +90deg (folded, towards -Y) -> 0 (raised, f20) ... 0 (f40) -> +90deg (f55).
   Handle_Pivot_B is the mirror: -90deg -> 0 -> -90deg.  Handle_GripPoint = centre of the finger window (hand anchor).

2) OVER-CENTRE DRAW LATCHES  (Latch_Pivot_1/2 + Latch_Bail_1/2; frames 60..90)
   Per latch: base on the body (Latch_Base), a lever hanging from an axis just under the seam (Latch_Lever on
   Latch_Pivot), a steel bail hinged to the lever 16 mm below that axis (Latch_Bail_Mesh on Latch_Bail), and a catch pin
   on the lid (Latch_Catches). Closed: the bail hook sits over the catch pin and the bail pivot lies 3 mm on the wall side
   of the line axis->catch (over-centre), so the pull holds the lever against the wall and presses the lid down.
   Opening: the lever swings outward about 85 deg; the bail first follows (the lid recovers 1.8 mm, f60..70), at about
   50 deg the hook lets go of the pin and the bail drops free. Frames are keyed every frame from the linkage geometry.
   Latch_Pivot_k rotates about -X (negative = outward).

3) HERMETIC LID SEAL  (Lid_Seat, Lid_Gasket; Lid_Rim, Body_Ledge)
   The lid has an inward flange (Lid_Rim) with a groove; the EPDM gasket (Lid_Gasket) sits in the groove and stands
   1.8 mm proud. The body has a matching ledge (Body_Ledge). Latched, the lid sits 1.8 mm lower and the gasket is
   squeezed against the ledge (scale Z 0.69); released, the lid rises 1.8 mm (Lid_Seat translation) and the gasket
   returns to full height (scale Z 1.0). Only then does the lid open (Lid_Pivot, 0 -> -110 deg, f79..120).

ORDER OF USE: unfold handle -> carry -> fold -> release both latches (lid rises off the gasket) -> open lid. Reverse to close.

ANCHORS: TubeSlot_01..32 (seat of each tube bottom, 8 x 4, pitch 32 x 40 mm), Tube_01..32, TubeGrid_Origin,
Handle_GripPoint, Latch_Pivot_1/2, Lid_Pivot.
MATERIALS: Case_HDPE, Latch_Yellow (latches, handle, brackets base), Gasket_EPDM, Hinge_Steel (pins, bails, springs, hinges),
Rubber_Grip, Tray_PP, Label_Area, LabGlass_Material.
