# Authored receiving tube geometry audit

## Asset measurement and coordinate system

`node tests/measure-model.mjs public/assets/models/LabTestTube.glb` measures the authored `ContentsVolume` bounds as x/z = -0.007 to +0.007 m and y = 0.0005 to 0.0555 m. `tests/night/wave14g1/measure-vertices.mjs` groups actual mesh vertices by y: the radius rises from zero at 0.0005 m to 0.007 m at 0.0075 m, following a spherical hemisphere, then stays 0.007 m through 0.0555 m. Thus the rendered liquid body has a 7 mm radius, a 7 mm hemispherical bottom and 55 mm of height measured from its bottom. The mesh has scale (1,1,1). `Tube_Glass` bounds are x/z = +/-0.0098 m and y = 0 to 0.15 m. Its straight bore has 7.5 mm radius from y about 0.009 to 0.1477 m, with a 9 to 9.8 mm outer radius and a lip at 0.15 m. The glass shell therefore extends far above the authored 55 mm liquid body. The difference between the 7 mm rendered liquid and the 7.5 mm bore is a 0.5 mm visual clearance, not evidence for the old 3.85 mm effective radius.

The game uses these asset coordinates directly. `src/scenes/laboratory/ui/StandStation.tsx:97` states the 0.15 m mouth height, and lines 127-134 publish the clamped root's `matrixWorld` into `CLAMP_TUBE_MOUTH.root`. `src/scenes/laboratory/ui/InspectableTestTubeSlot.tsx:387,479,585` uses scale 1 for occupied tubes and line 556 applies the group's world scale to the mouth offset. Empty rack holes alone shrink (`:431,444`). `src/scenes/laboratory/ui/PourStream.tsx:121-124` transforms the local surface through the published root matrix. These paths support one world unit per metre for an occupied tube. A live clamped/hand world matrix was not captured, so no exact translated world position is asserted.

## Volume and surfaces

For height h above the liquid body's 0.0005 m bottom and r = 0.007 m, A(h) = pi(2rh-h²) for 0 <= h <= r and pi r² for r <= h <= 0.055 m. Integration gives V(h) = pi h²(r-h/3) for the bowl and 2 pi r³/3 + pi r²(h-r) for the cylinder. The game accepts at most 5 mL (`src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeAmounts.ts:6`), which maps to 34.814 mm; geometric capacity at 55 mm is about 8.108 mL. The 2.5 mL surface is 18.574 mm above the liquid bottom. All formulas and bounds are in `src/scenes/laboratory/lib/vesselGeometry.ts:12-15,17-22,57-74`.

Previously `tubeEffectiveRadius` found the fictitious radius, about 3.849 mm, that places 2.5 mL at the authored 55 mm top. This was an artistic fill calibration: `src/scenes/laboratory/lib/testTubeContents.ts:128-130` also defines every nonempty authored mesh as full. It is inconsistent with the 7 mm rendered liquid body and with the 7 mm field mask. The legacy height and physical volume it occupies in the 7 mm cavity are tabulated in [mapping.md](mapping.md); at 2.5 mL the old mask represents about 8.107 mL. The new interpretation treats stored game millilitres as actual millilitres within the 7 mm rendered liquid body. This is coherent among storage, visible height and field geometry, though it retains the authored 0.5 mm clearance from the shell bore.

`src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeReactionVisuals.ts:486-504` measures the mesh in its own coordinates, uses its bottom as the field origin, and creates a rounded wall through the 55 mm mesh extent (`:553-569`). The field texture extends only to the 5 mL upright height, 34.814 mm (`:510-512`), while the visual wall retains the 55 mm authored height. The visible plane is `dot(up,p)=level`, where `level` is computed from `bottom + size.y * fill` (`:1216`) and given to the fields (`:1327,:1389,:1450`) and silver shader (`:1501`). The shader's `uRadius`, `uBottom`, `uHeight` come from the same measured radius, bottom and field height (`:737`, `src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverVolumeMaterial.ts:35-36`); its bowl test is at `:97-98`. `LiquidMask.point` maps voxel centres to [-radius,+radius] in x/z and [bottom,bottom+height] in y (`src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeVolumeField.ts:43-51`), with the same hemispherical predicate at `:53-61`. Silver runtime's aliases now resolve to the canonical radius and 5 mL field height (`src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverGeometry.ts:4-8`). `tubePourImpact.ts:95-107` and `PourStream.tsx:121-124` still call the compatibility functions; those functions now delegate to the canonical geometry. G2 owns direct migration of those call sites.

The authored `ContentsVolume` is not shortened by scaling its mesh along y. `tubeReactionVisuals` substitutes a wall, cap, and level shader and sets `fill = heightFromVolume(ml)/size.y` (`:1167-1170`). This clips the replacement visual at the new surface. Standalone rack previews still use the authored full body because they have no measured amount identity (`:590-593`). `tubeVisualFill` now returns the same height ratio, though production rendering calculates it directly.

## Scope of numerical geometry

`LiquidMask.cellVolumeMl` assigns sampled wet voxel volume and normalizes the active-cell sum to the analytic stored volume. Scalar arrays in the current fields are *amounts* (`SpeciesField.total` sums raw values at `tubeVolumeField.ts:100-106`), not concentrations, so regridding moves those amounts to valid cells without multiplying by the changed voxel volume. This preserves the existing serialized/diagnostic amount convention. A future concentration representation must use `n_i = c_i * cellVolumeMl[i]` and perform volume-aware remapping.

For tilted fields, the current visual plane keeps the `dot(up,p)=level` convention and voxel fractions sample its intersection. The normalized wet-volume sum remains the canonical amount, but the plane's geometric intercept is not solved from a three-dimensional tilted-volume integral. Exact free-surface volume under tilt remains an integration task shared with the shader/stream geometry; this audit does not claim that the tilted silhouette encloses exactly the ledger volume.
