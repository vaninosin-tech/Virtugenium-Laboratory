# Wave 18 F3: sectional AgCl population and mass accounting

## Result and stop condition

The model now has 13 conservative aggregate size sections, each advected by resolved flow and its own porous-sphere slip. The 260-node limit no longer holds aggregate mass in a fine-carrier seed field. Nevertheless, the requested millimetre cotton does **not** form by 22 s. Both real-pour grids have **zero visible nodes**, so the largest connected structure, bounding box, and radius of gyration are all zero. The collision model moves substantial material out of fine AgCl, but almost all of it remains below 3 micrometres. This triggers the task's stop condition. No collision or brightness parameter was changed to force a visual pass. These are numerical results, not browser observations.

The limiting diagnosis is **B, mass trapped in unresolved small size classes**, combined with **D, growth through the specified Brownian and resolved-shear kernels is too slow to reach millimetre sizes in 22 s**. It is not A: 0.830 of 0.969 formed unit is aggregated at 20 s LOW. C cannot be judged with no promoted structures; F4 can inspect the additive `visibleFlocs` and wave18 dumps but has no millimetre geometry from this physics run. E is also present: only 0.0064 unit has settled at 20 s LOW. The fine optical depth falls to 2.13 at 20 s, so persistent thick milkiness is not established numerically either.

## Existing implementation, answered from code

Before this change, primary-primary collisions in [`advanceFlocSystem`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverField.ts) moved fine mass to `aggregateSeeds`; a cell needed `1e-4` unit before [`addMass`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/flocSystem.ts) was called. Wave 16's `FLOC_MIN_PARCEL=5e-6` had already been removed by wave 17. The default node quantum was `0.03` normalized unit at 2.1 mm radius ([`flocSystem.ts:51`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/flocSystem.ts#L51)), although the old seed threshold allowed much smaller actual node masses. A node grew by adding the parcel to nearby node mass if the old node was below three quanta, or by spawning a new linked node ([`flocSystem.ts:234`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/flocSystem.ts#L234)). Unlinked approaching nodes merged on contact with mass-weighted centre and velocity, links redirected and indices rebuilt ([`flocSystem.ts:315`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/flocSystem.ts#L315), [`flocSystem.ts:364`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/flocSystem.ts#L364)); this iteration also makes compaction mass weighted.

The hydrodynamic envelope radius is `r0 (mass/quantum)^(1/Df)` with `Df=1.9`, and solid fraction is `phi0 (r/r0)^(Df-3)` ([`flocSystem.ts:91`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/flocSystem.ts#L91)). The renderer receives the rasterized node mass and visual tufts/bridges, while `aggregateTexture.R` receives conserved node plus seed density ([`flocSystem.ts:599`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/flocSystem.ts#L599), [`tubeSilverField.ts:580`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverField.ts#L580)). At 260 nodes, `addMass` rejected new parcels, leaving mass in `aggregateSeeds`; those seeds followed the fine carrier transport and lacked their own slip. Nodes deposit only at floor contact, with a slip-limited fraction into `SilverSedimentField.deposit` ([`flocSystem.ts:511`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/flocSystem.ts#L511)). Wave 17 LOW had 0.1220 of 0.1941 aggregate unit in that seed state at 20 s. These are the reasons a sizeable aggregate budget did not make sizeable objects.

## Stage 3 chemistry and scale

The shared reagent configuration has Ag+ `1.15` and Cl- `1.0` in the 1 mL portions ([`tubeAmounts.ts:28`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeAmounts.ts#L28)). The realism documents specify the reaction but give no reason for a 15% silver excess. I treat it as a legacy gameplay amount, not an intentional chemical concentration ratio. Aggregation-enabled stage 3 defaults to equimolar `1.0/1.0`; `__silverAgg=0` retains `1.15/1.0` for the accepted no-aggregation path. The constructor still accepts an explicit `1.15` for an excess scenario. No shared reagent configuration was edited.

One stage 3 unit is the AgCl from 1 mL of 0.1 mol/L NaCl: 0.1 mmol, 14.332 mg, and `2.57770e-9 m3` compact solid at 5560 kg/m3 ([`tubeSilverSediment.ts:5`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverSediment.ts#L5)). This volume feeds primary number density, section characteristic mass, floc solid fraction, slip, and sediment packing. At completion, equimolar Ag+ and Cl- approach zero; the legacy excess would leave at least `0.15` unit Ag+ (`0.015 mmol`) after all chloride reacts. At 22 s LOW, the equimolar run has extent `0.972784`, so each dissolved reactant has `0.027216` unit (`0.002722 mmol`) left. The per-step tests verify `Ag+ + extent = initial Ag+`, `Cl- + extent = delivered Cl-`, and all solid states sum to extent. The old golden extents are not applicable because silver loading and aggregate feedback changed.

The stage 3 quantum is now derived from a compact 1 micrometre crossover with packing fraction 0.5 and `Df=1.9` above it: `0.0016674` unit at 2.1 mm, with solid fraction `1.108e-4` ([`tubeSilverField.ts:41`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverField.ts#L41)). The same radius-mass law applies from the larger sections to nodes. A 1 mm radius therefore requires about `0.000406` unit, rather than an arbitrary visual radius increase. The compact crossover is an explicit model assumption, not a measured AgCl cluster morphology.

## Section scheme, transport, and ownership of mass

[`SectionalPopulation`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/sectionalPopulation.ts) has 13 radius pivots from 0.32 micrometre to 1.763 mm, a factor of 2.05 between pivots. It stores normalized mass per section per cell; particle number is `section mass / characteristic particle mass`, and number density divides that by liquid cell volume. Primary collisions move exactly their consumed fine mass to section 0. Each section pair collides by the Brownian plus `G(ri+rj)^3/3` shear kernel, with the same local electrolyte-dependent sticking probability as fine particles. A bounded fixed-pivot split sends the product mass to adjacent pivots; pair source loss equals product mass exactly. The collision update admits at most one newly reached pivot per step in normal operation and does not generate a node by changing radius alone.

Each section has its own terminal slip from effective density `1000 + phi(5560-1000)`, Brinkman-screened Stokes drag and iterative Reynolds correction ([`flocSystem.ts:101`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/night/stage1/flocSystem.ts#L101)). Conservative face fluxes use the solver's resolved cell velocity plus vertical slip. Bottom-contact section mass enters the sediment field; dry-cell remapping follows the liquid mask. The real grids are 12 x 24 x 12 LOW and 16 x 32 x 16 HIGH in this code, rather than the 22 x 44 x 22 stated in the request ([`tubeSilverField.ts:185`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverField.ts#L185)). Thirteen mass arrays and thirteen transfer arrays use 208 bytes per grid cell, approximately 0.72 MB LOW and 1.70 MB HIGH, excluding renderer textures, solver arrays and object overhead. Collision work is bounded by 91 pairs per wet cell in the full 13-class case, with the occupied-class ceiling reducing the measured replay cost; transport considers six faces per class.

A section particle can promote when its characteristic radius is at least one quarter of the smaller horizontal or vertical grid spacing and its cell holds at least one particle mass ([`tubeSilverField.ts:941`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverField.ts#L941)). Each promotion removes one characteristic particle mass, not the whole cell population, and adds that exact mass to a linked `FlocSystem` node. At the cap, promotion fails and the mass stays in the moving, settling section. The one-node synthetic test exercises this overflow and checks the ledger. Existing nodes advect with resolved flow, rotate and deform through plastic link relaxation, may separate under the configured stretch condition, and settle by their size-dependent drag. Breakage is kept off by default (`__silverBreak=1` enables it); with zero real-pour nodes, this replay cannot justify turning it on. Shear erosion returns material to section 0, not an invisible tint class. Legacy `dissolveSmoke` remains disabled in stage 3 unless `__silverSmokeToTint` is explicitly set. The compatibility `aggregateSeeds` array remains zero after section initialization; it is not a fourth reservoir.

The conserved ledger is `sum(suspension[0..2]) + coarse + section masses + nodes + pending + sediment = extent`. [`assertMassInvariant`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverField.ts#L984) executes after every stage 3 fixed step with tolerance `1e-12`; the replay's largest error was `2.08e-14` LOW and `6.28e-14` HIGH. `aggregateTexture.R` contains section plus visible-node mass, which is removed from fine `texture`; G and B are mass-weighted looseness and compaction, A stays zero. [`visibleFlocs`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverField.ts#L128) and `dumpAggregateState()` add component bounds, mass, radius of gyration, node IDs, section masses, radii, and sediment fill diagnostics without changing existing field names. The wave18-labeled state dumps at 1, 2, 3, 4.5, 6, 9, 12, 14, 17, 20 and 22 s are in [`wave16shared`](../wave16shared/).

The sediment field stores only actual normalized solid mass. Bulk volume is compact volume divided by `1-porosity`; porosity begins at 0.90 and compacts only when deposited load produces pressure ([`tubeSilverSediment.ts:24`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverSediment.ts#L24), [`tubeSilverSediment.ts:51`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverSediment.ts#L51)). ## Lower-liquid tracer and density

The passive-tracer control sets `__silverDiag='tracer'` and disables precipitation. The lower third is defined relative to the actual 1 to 2 mL liquid height, not one third of the 55 mm cavity. Its mean tracer concentration first exceeds 1% of the full-liquid mean at **0.40 s**. The lower/full mean ratio is `0.087` at 1 s, `0.332` at 3 s, `0.573` at 14 s, and `0.651` at 20 s. It does not remain tracer-free long after the pour in this model. RMS resolved velocity falls from about 1.06 mm/s at 3 s to 0.11 mm/s at 20 s in the aggregation-off flow. Fine settling cannot explain the tracer result because the tracer has no settling. The headless run cannot rule in or out a separate renderer limitation in the real game.

Published density measurements cover 0.1 mol/kg NaCl near 25 C, about `1.001 g/mL`, and a 0.1 M AgNO3 solution is about `1.01-1.012 g/mL` at room temperature ([NaCl experimental study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0021961482900805), [AgNO3 density study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0040603178851405), [0.1 M product specification](https://www.merckmillipore.com/deepweb/assets/sigmaaldrich/product/documents/184/337/109081-technical-data-sheet-silbernitrat-0-1m-36monate.pdf)). These are approximate cross-source values, not a matched-temperature precision pair; they suggest the receiving AgNO3 is roughly 0.8-1.1% denser and the NaCl-on-top arrangement is gravitationally stable. Using 1.0119 and 1.0012 g/mL at 25 C gives `g'≈0.104 m/s2`. The model's default `__silverBuoy=0.1` with `loadStrength=0.005` corresponds to only `g'≈0.0005 m/s2` per unit local tracer, about 200 times weaker ([`tubeSilverField.ts:142`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverField.ts#L142), [`tubeSilverField.ts:416`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeSilverField.ts#L416), [`tubeFlowSolver.ts:166`](../../../src/scenes/laboratory/lib/tubeFlowSolver.ts#L166)). At 2 mL height 15.3 mm and measured 14 s RMS speed about 0.61 mm/s, the bulk `Ri=g'H/U²` is about `4300` for that density estimate and about `21` for the modeled buoyancy. Local jet Richardson number will differ. The fast lower-tracer penetration therefore points to weak modeled density contrast and/or too much modeled circulation or eddy transport; it is not evidence that the physically stable layer must disappear. Solver and rendering changes are outside F3 ownership.

## Performance, verification and integration

On this host, one 50 ms field step measured LOW aggregation on `p50 14.7`, `p95 24.7`, peak `42.9` ms versus off `3.9/7.8/23.6` ms. HIGH was `32.7/57.5/333.3` ms versus off `8.8/19.4/41.7` ms. These are headless CPU timings with host-load sensitivity, not game FPS. The HIGH cost is a hardware-budget risk relative to the reported 44 median and 33 tenth-percentile FPS during pouring. Mass arrays alone use 0.36 MB LOW and 0.85 MB HIGH; fixed collision/transport scratch doubles that. Texture CPU arrays add 55,872 and 132,096 bytes. Measured heap deltas include garbage collection and do not establish a per-frame allocation count. `FlocSystem` still makes transient objects; no zero-allocation claim is made.

The no-aggregation path retains legacy `1.15` silver, the legacy sediment-volume default, and no section transfer. Its stored 1, 2, 3, 4.5 and 6 s LOW/HIGH extents match the accepted off replay exactly; summed fine masses differ by at most floating-point last bits in the checked rows, so a full byte-for-byte field-state comparison has **not** been proven. Flags `__silverJetModel` and `__silverSubgrid` remain off. The two old goldens in `wave4a/field.test.ts` and `wave6perf/flocPerf.test.ts` were not re-pinned. The former is the previously reported stage-one raft threshold; the latter expects the rejected floc calibration. Their final observed results and the whole-suite count are recorded below after the final suite run.
