# Wave 4A: dense AgCl physics

## Changes

- Stage 1 partitions every new AgCl solid amount into 45% dense wet curd and 55% fine suspension. Both reservoirs remain conserved; the fine fraction is not drawn as stage-1 cotton. The prior stage-1 test's zero-suspension assumption was updated.
- Air-bearing curd rises by a conservative buoyant flux and spreads laterally between wet surface cells. Trapped air decays with a 40 s wetting time. Dense settling is hindered by trapped air and concentration.
- The floor pile relaxes through fractional column heights and conservative transfers to all four neighbours. Its local target is 18 degrees, leaving margin under the requested 35-degree bound during deposition. Maximum packing is 2.5 normalized concentration.
- Removed the temporary `window.__silverFieldDebug` assignment. The existing tuning getters remain for review: `__silverLoad` defaults to 0.005 m/s² per concentration, `__silverPack` to 2.5, `__silverJet` to 0.1, `__silverMix` to 0.00004, and `__silverEddy` to 0.8. Only stage 1 uses the revised load, pack and jet defaults.
