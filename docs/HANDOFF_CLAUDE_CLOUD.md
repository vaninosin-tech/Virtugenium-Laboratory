## 4. Current results (what is verified and what is not)
Verified in the real game (Playwright, real `runStandAction` pour): delivery first valid impact about 600 ms after the click, tracer and Cl- appear in the same step; Ag+ + AgCl and Cl- + AgCl conserved to about 1e-9 or better; source container volume decreases by the emitted amount; receiving volume rises 1 to 2 mL; no field before the first valid impact; stream endpoint matches the ledger impact point (6.5 mm from the axis, 0.5 mm from the inner wall: a deliberate game geometry, unchanged); shaders compile (145 programs link), pour FPS about 44 median and 33 at the 10th percentile with the accepted cloud (no aggregation).
Results by stage (artifacts are NOT in git; they are on the user's PC under `D:\Claude\virtugenium-night\diagnosticC\`: videos and contact sheets):
- Corrected geometry alone, no flags, aggregation off: AgCl 0.09 at 1 s, 0.29 at 2 s, 0.71 at 6 s, 0.85 at 9 s, 0.94 at 14 s; spatial contrast stays non-uniform; milky cloud from a curling plume at the impact wall; visible on the lab wall. This is the user-accepted fine-suspension FOUNDATION.
- Aggregation (wave 16 to 19): physics is conserved, but no visible floc nodes ever form (zero nodes) and the deposit is very thin (about 0.1 mg: 0.0011 mm uniform layer, correctly derived: the 0.17 mm figure in some reports is the FULL 14.3 mg deposit). Most aggregate mass stays in sub-3-micrometre sections: wave 19 sensitivity grid (primary radius 0.02 to 0.2 micrometre times sticking 0.05 to 1) found no material large aggregate by 22 s: outcome "the current population model cannot produce large flocs on this timescale; visible cotton must rely on transported concentrated suspension structures". Selected most defensible configuration: `window.__silverPrimaryRadiusUm=0.05; window.__silverStickingScale=1;` with `window.__silverAgg=1`. In the real game it gives dense S-shaped white plumes with clear liquid beside them at 1.5 to 4 s and a uniformly milky liquid by 9 to 18 s; no separate cotton clumps; sediment not visible; fine mass almost zero, the milk is carried by the aggregate sections.
- KNOWN NUMERICAL DEFECT (important): `sectionalPopulation.ts` retains about 2.03 times the analytic particle number on a homogeneous Smoluchowski box test (the fixed-pivot split loses only 0.23 particle per collision; the first pivot is always 0.32 micrometre so small primaries skip intermediate sizes). It biases growth of large classes DOWN. A replacement scheme validated against analytic mass and number solutions is the necessary next physics step before concluding that large flocs are impossible. Report: `docs/night/wave19s1/REPORT.md`.
- Other unresolved: HIGH grid aggregation cost was reduced to about 19 ms per 50 ms step (was 31); the clear lower third of the liquid seen in early runs disappears in the aggregation runs; the dotted moire on the sediment: a CPU diagnostic shows a large reduction but it is not confirmed in the browser (the deposit is too thin to see); experiments 1, 3, 4 were checked only statically; jet model and subgrid remain off.
